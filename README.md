# Orbit Merge

A juicy, instantly-playable casual **merge game** — drop glowing orbs into the jar,
two of the same kind merge into a bigger one, and you chain your way up to the **Star**.
Don't let the jar overflow!

## Play

Just open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no dependencies —
one self-contained file. Works on desktop (mouse / keyboard) and mobile (touch).

## How to play

- **Move** your finger or mouse to aim the falling orb.
- **Release / click / Space** to drop it.
- Two orbs of the **same tier merge** into the next, bigger tier and score points.
- Reach the top tier (★) for a big bonus.
- If settled orbs pile up above the red line, the jar **overflows** — game over.

## Features

- Custom lightweight 2D circle physics (gravity, collisions, restitution).
- 10 merge tiers with a rainbow-to-gold color ramp.
- Particle bursts, floating score popups, screen shake, and WebAudio sound (toggleable).
- Best score saved locally between sessions.
- Fully responsive canvas — scales to phone or desktop.
- Keyboard controls: ← → to nudge, Space to drop, Enter to restart.

Made with ♥ — no frameworks, no assets, pure HTML/CSS/JS.
